STEP 01

Write down everything you’re carrying.

Your partner wants one thing, a customer asked for another, your gut says a third. Nobody’s pushing a bad idea. Get every one of them out of your head, including the ones you’ve been avoiding, and note who it came from.

STEP 02 + 03

Put every idea on one of three lists. Then put Next in order.

Now is this cycle, and gets dates because you’ve decided. Next is in order, no dates: you know what comes first, not when. Later has no order and no dates. It isn’t a no. It’s a not yet.

THE TRAP

Dates on everything.

Hold the line

Partners want dates on Next and Later. Staff want them. A lender wants quarters across the top of the page. A date on Now is a commitment. A date anywhere else is a guess, and people will treat it as a promise. What hurts trust isn’t missing dates. It’s dates that keep moving. When someone asks, say it plainly:

“We know the order. We don’t know the timing yet, and we’d rather tell you that than promise something we’ll have to take back.”

STEP 04

Take the idea somebody keeps pushing. Put it where it honestly belongs.

Write the reason beside it, and show that person the page. The answer usually isn’t a no. It’s later, with a reason. That conversation is the point.

This prints next to the idea on your roadmap.
